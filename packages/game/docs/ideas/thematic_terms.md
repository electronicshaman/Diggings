# Thematic Terms & Inspiration

This document stores unused concepts and terms discussed during development for future inspiration (events, new mechanics, lore).

## Possible Card Prefixes

We chose **Eldritch** for the highest rarity, but these terms fit the Occult/Lovecraftian theme well:

- **Forbidden**: Suggests knowledge that is dangerous to possess. Good for curse cards or high-risk spells.
- **Arcane**: Suggests deep, hidden magic. Good for skill-heavy or intelligence-based effects.
- **Profane**: Suggests unholy acts. Good for sacrificial mechanics or corruption.
- **Anomalous**: Suggests weird science or reality-bending effects.
- **Spectral**: Suggests ghosts and spirits. Good for ethereal or exhaust mechanics.
- **Fabled**: Suggests local legends and tall tales.
- **Pristine**: Suggests untouched or perfect quality (maybe for resources).
- **Unspeakable**: Directly references Lovecraftian tropes of descriptions beyond human language.
- **Nameless**: Suggests an entity so alien or ancient it lacks a human identifier.
- **Blasphemous**: Implies a violation of natural or moral laws, perfect for high-risk cards.
- **Abyssal**: Connects the deep mining themes of the Gold Rush with the cosmic depths of the Void.
- **Unfathomable**: Highlights the scale and incomprehensibility of cosmic horror.
- **Preternatural**: An archaic term for things existing outside the normal natural order.
- **Cosmic**: A direct nod to the genre, emphasizing the scale of the threat.
- **Cursed**: Blends the superstitions of the goldfields with malevolent eldritch influence.
- **Void-Touched**: Directly links cards to the game's core "Void" corruption mechanic.
- **Incursion**: Represents reality being breached by the eldritch madness of the Outback.
