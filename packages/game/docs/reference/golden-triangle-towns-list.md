## Map Generation Notes

### Distance References (from Melbourne)

- Ballarat: 105km west-northwest
- Bendigo: 150km north-northwest  
- Castlemaine: 123km northwest
- Maryborough: 200km northwest
- Bacchus Marsh: 50km west
- Kyneton: 86km north

### Terrain Features to Include

- **Rivers**: Yarrowee (Ballarat), Bendigo Creek, Forest Creek, Loddon River
- **Mountains**: Mount Alexander, Mount Buninyong, Mount Warrenheip
- **Roads**: Main Road (Melbourne-Ballarat), Sydney Road (Melbourne-Bendigo)
- **Tracks**: Cobb & Co routes between all major centers

### Settlement Patterns

- Major centers have satellite towns within 5-10km
- Chinese camps typically on outskirts or separate areas
- Supply towns along main routes at 20-30km intervals
- Mining camps cluster around water sources
