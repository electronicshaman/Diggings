## Curios (Cross-Class Relics)

### Common Curios (Simple, Universal)

- **Lucky Nugget**: +3 starting gold each combat
- **Worn Boots**: First movement each day costs 0 time
- **Old Compass**: Reveal 1 additional adjacent node
- **Thick Leather**: +1 block whenever you play a Defend
- **Sharpened Blade**: +1 damage to all Strike cards
- **Canvas Bag**: +1 card draw at start of combat
- **Rabbit's Foot**: 10% chance to dodge any attack

### Rare Curios (Build-Defining)

- **Fool's Gold Crown**: All gold gains doubled, all gold costs doubled
- **Corrupted Compass**: Can move to any revealed node, take 5 corruption
- **Temporal Pocket Watch**: First card each turn returns to hand
- **Blood Money**: Gain 5 gold whenever you take damage
- **Gunslinger's Belt**: +2 ammo at start of combat (great for Bushranger)
- **Prospector's Monocle**: See all gold values on map
- **Endless Bottle**: Brew tokens don't disappear between combats

### Legendary Curios (Run-Altering)

- **The Antipodean Star**: Walk through void nodes, reality sometimes inverts
- **Ned Kelly's Helm**: Immune to first damage each combat, -1 card draw
- **Eureka License**: All mines are already revealed and connected
- **The Last Drop**: All cards Ferment twice as fast, max HP -10

### Corrupted Curios (Risk/Reward)

- **Void-Touched Coin**: +10 gold per combat, gain 1 corruption per combat
- **Eldritch Map**: See entire graph, sanity drains while moving
- **Cursed Pickaxe**: Double damage to elites, all cards cost +1
- **Whispering Flask**: +2 Brew per turn, randomly exhausts cards

## How Curios Interact with Classes

**Same Curio, Different Value:**

**"Temporal Pocket Watch"** (First card returns to hand):

- **Prospector**: Play expensive gold cards twice
- **Bushranger**: Double-tap momentum cards
- **Tracker**: Setup cards become incredible
- **Publican**: Fermented cards easier to save

**"Blood Money"** (Gain gold when damaged):

- **Prospector**: Directly fuels damage engine
- **Bushranger**: Nice bonus but not build-defining
- **Tracker**: Helps recover from failed setups
- **Publican**: Synergizes with self-damage drinks
