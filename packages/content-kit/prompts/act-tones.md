# Act Tones

Four-act narrative arc. Each act has tonal expectations and sensory palette. Inject into stage prompts.

## Act 1 — Arrival
- **Tone**: Hope, opportunity, frontier grit
- **Sensory palette**: dust, sun, sweat, canvas, hammered iron
- **Mood**: hard work, possibility, rough camaraderie
- **Threat register**: human-scale (cheating, injury, weather)

## Act 2 — Fever
- **Tone**: Greed, competition, first wrongness
- **Sensory palette**: gold glint, bloodshot eyes, sour beer, mud
- **Mood**: paranoia, escalation, frayed bonds
- **Threat register**: violence between people, first eldritch hints

## Act 3 — Blasphemy
- **Tone**: Forbidden truths, isolation, corruption
- **Sensory palette**: salt, silence, shifting geometry, wrong shadows
- **Mood**: dread, sanity erosion, taboo broken
- **Threat register**: the changed, the contaminated, the cursed

## Act 4 — Unmaking
- **Tone**: Cosmic dread, futility, transformation
- **Sensory palette**: static, void, impossible colors, time-slip
- **Mood**: existential collapse, becoming-something-else
- **Threat register**: reality itself, identity loss, ego dissolution
