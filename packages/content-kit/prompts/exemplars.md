# Prose Exemplars

Calibration examples for show-vs-tell.

## Good
> The publican's smile doesn't reach his eyes. His hands stay below the bar.

Why: concrete physical detail (smile, hands, bar). Implies suspicion without naming it. Reader infers menace.

## Bad
> The innkeeper looks suspicious and might be hiding something.

Why: tells the reader the conclusion. No sensory detail. Generic.

## Rule
Show through concrete detail. Don't tell through summary.

## Anti-clichés (avoid)
- "Indian burial ground" / generic Indigenous-spirit horror
- "It was a dark and stormy night"
- Direct exposition of monster lore by NPCs
- Adverb-stacked menace ("ominously", "menacingly", "darkly")
