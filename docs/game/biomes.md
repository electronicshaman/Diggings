## Township
**Atmosphere:** desperate commerce, barely‑held order  
**Sensory:** canvas flap, mud squelch, tobacco smoke, coins clinking, distant hammering  
**Dangers:** debt collectors, licensing troopers, claim‑jumper rumors, sly grog poisoning  
**Voice:** transactions and threats; trust is currency.  
**Antipatterns:** wild west, saloon brawl, sheriff, frontier justice

**Act presence (1–4):** Arrival 4 • Fever 2 • Blasphemy 1 • Unmaking 2

## The Diggings
**Atmosphere:** feverish labor, jealous eyes  
**Sensory:** pick strikes, turned earth smell, sweat‑stink, gold glint, creaking windlass  
**Dangers:** cave‑ins, claim disputes, exhaustion, the colour madness  
**Voice:** obsession made physical; every shovelful might be fortune or nothing.  
**Antipatterns:** gold rush fever, struck it rich, eureka

**Act presence (1–4):** Arrival 3 • Fever 4 • Blasphemy 2 • Unmaking 1

## The Bush
**Atmosphere:** watchful silence, ancient indifference  
**Sensory:** eucalyptus oil, cicada drone, cracking twigs, heat shimmer, crow calls  
**Dangers:** bushrangers, getting lost, snakebite, thirst, things that watch  
**Voice:** the land doesn’t care if you live; survival is earned.  
**Antipatterns:** deadly Australia, everything wants to kill you, outback

**Act presence (1–4):** Arrival 2 • Fever 3 • Blasphemy 3 • Unmaking 2

## The Mines
**Atmosphere:** suffocating wrongness, depth that thinks  
**Sensory:** dripping water, candle flicker, stone groaning, stale air, echo distortion  
**Dangers:** collapse, foul air, whispers, losing the way up, what you dig into  
**Voice:** claustrophobia and creeping dread; deeper means wronger.  
**Antipatterns:** canary in coal mine, dig too deep, dwarven, ancient evil

**Act presence (1–4):** Arrival 1 • Fever 3 • Blasphemy 4 • Unmaking 2

## The Waste
**Atmosphere:** poisoned stillness, memory of failure  
**Sensory:** cracked earth, chemical taste, bone‑white sun, silence pools, rust smell  
**Dangers:** contamination, ghost‑touched, dehydration, the past repeating  
**Voice:** everything here failed or was abandoned; the ghosts remember why.  
**Antipatterns:** post‑apocalyptic, nuclear, zombie, wasteland warrior

**Act presence (1–4):** Arrival 0 • Fever 2 • Blasphemy 4 • Unmaking 3

## The Scar
**Atmosphere:** reality bleeding, geometry lies  
**Sensory:** static taste, colors unnamed, shadow lag, ground ripple, time‑slip nausea  
**Dangers:** transformation, losing yourself, the pull, seeing too much  
**Voice:** wrongness made mundane; impossibility described matter‑of‑factly.  
**Antipatterns:** portal, dimension, tentacles, eldritch abomination, sanity meter

**Act presence (1–4):** Arrival 0 • Fever 1 • Blasphemy 3 • Unmaking 4

## Sacred Site
**Atmosphere:** ancient weight, watching presence  
**Sensory:** ochre smell, stone warmth, wind voices, star clarity, bone‑deep vibration  
**Dangers:** spiritual debt, taboo violation, ancestor judgment, power offered  
**Voice:** reverence and consequence; this place was here before and will remain after.  
**Antipatterns:** burial ground, ancient curse, mystical native, spirit guide, noble savage, magic indian

**Act presence (1–4):** Arrival 1 • Fever 2 • Blasphemy 3 • Unmaking 4

## The River
**Atmosphere:** deceptive calm, hidden current  
**Sensory:** water slap, fish rot, rope creak, mist chill, reflected light  
**Dangers:** drowning, river pirates, what surfaces, the ferryman’s price  
**Voice:** movement and transaction; the river takes as much as it gives.  
**Antipatterns:** river styx, ferryman of death, river monster

**Act presence (1–4):** Arrival 2 • Fever 3 • Blasphemy 3 • Unmaking 2
