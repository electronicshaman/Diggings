# Theme & Setting Summary

## Core Identity
- **Genre:** Roguelite card battler
- **Setting:** 1850s Australian gold rush meets Lovecraftian/cosmic horror
- **Tone:** Australian gothic — harsh frontier survival, greed, superstition, and creeping dread

## The World
The goldfields are a frontier pressure‑cooker: boomtowns appear overnight, law is thin, and the land itself feels wrong. Dig sites crack open ancient seams that should have remained buried. The deeper you go, the more the veil frays.

## Key Themes
- **Greed vs. survival** — gold brings fortune and ruin in equal measure
- **Isolation** — the bush is vast, uncaring, and full of unseen eyes
- **Corruption** — physical, spiritual, and psychological decay are inseparable
- **Faith vs. madness** — belief can steady the mind or hasten collapse

## Mood & Atmosphere
- Dust, heat, and exhaustion
- Flickering lanterns in tunnels, echoing drips, and distant whispers
- Uneasy towns: saloons, camps, and half‑built settlements
- Supernatural hints that are never fully explained

## Mechanical Echoes of Theme
- **Dual failure states:** Health + Sanity
- **Persistent corruption:** long‑term contamination across a run
- **Class identity:** archetypes tied to gold‑rush history (Bushranger, Prospector, Tracker, Publican, Preacher)

## Player Experience Goal
A fast, tactical card battler where each encounter feels like a story beat in a larger descent — from gold fever to eldritch revelation.
