# The Mines

**Atmosphere:** suffocating wrongness, depth that thinks  
**Sensory:** dripping water, candle flicker, stone groaning, stale air, echo distortion  
**Dangers:** collapse, foul air, whispers, losing the way up, what you dig into  
**Voice:** claustrophobia and creeping dread; deeper means wronger.  
**Antipatterns:** canary in coal mine, dig too deep, dwarven, ancient evil

**Act presence (1–4):** Arrival 1 • Fever 3 • Blasphemy 4 • Unmaking 2
