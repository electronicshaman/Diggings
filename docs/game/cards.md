## Card Design Structure

Let's create a smart card pool that maximizes variety while minimizing development overhead.

## Common Card Pool (All Classes Can Find These)

### Basic Attacks (Simple, Reliable)

- **Strike**: 5 damage, 1 cost
- **Heavy Strike**: 8 damage, 2 cost
- **Quick Strike**: 3 damage, 0 cost
- **Wild Swing**: 4-8 damage, 1 cost

### Basic Defense

- **Defend**: 5 block, 1 cost
- **Heavy Guard**: 8 block, 2 cost
- **Dodge**: 3 block, 0 cost, Draw 1
- **Brace**: 2 block, gain 2 block next turn, 1 cost

### Basic Utility

- **Scout**: Draw 2 cards, 1 cost
- **Rest**: Heal 3 HP, Exhaust, 1 cost
- **Focus**: Next card costs 0, 0 cost
- **Desperate Measures**: Draw 3, Discard 2, 1 cost

### Common Status Effects (Simple, stackable)

- **Bleed**: Enemy takes 3 damage at turn end, 1 cost
- **Fortify**: Gain 4 block at turn start next turn, 1 cost
- **Rage**: +2 damage this turn, 1 cost
- **Cautious**: +2 block this turn, 1 cost

These 16 cards can appear in shops, rewards, and events for any class, making runs more varied without creating class-specific versions of basic effects.

## Class Starting Decks & Specialties

### PROSPECTOR - Starting Deck (10 cards)

**Basic Cards:**

- 3x **Prospect**: 3 damage, Gain 2 gold, 1 cost
- 2x **Defend**: 5 block, 1 cost
- 2x **Gold Rush**: Damage = gold/10, 1 cost

**Unique Starters:**

- 1x **Assay**: Look at top 3 cards, gain gold = their total cost, 1 cost
- 1x **Pay Dirt**: 7 damage, Costs 5 gold instead of energy, 0 cost
- 1x **Fool's Gold**: 5 block, If you have 0 gold: gain 10 gold, 1 cost

### Prospector Specialist Cards (Found during run)

**Common Class Cards:**

- **Gold Strike**: Deal damage = gold/5, lose 5 gold, 1 cost
- **Investment**: Lose all gold, gain double that amount next turn, 1 cost
- **Greed**: Draw cards = gold/20, 1 cost
- **Bribe**: Enemy loses their next turn, Costs 15 gold, 2 cost

**Rare Class Cards:**

- **Midas Touch**: ALL cards gain "Gain 1 gold when played" this combat, 2 cost
- **Mother Lode**: Gain 20 gold, Add 2 "Fool's Gold" curses to deck, 1 cost
- **Gold Fever**: Your attacks deal +1 damage per 10 gold (Power), 2 cost

**Unique Mechanic - Gold Costs**: Some cards cost gold instead of/in addition to energy, creating interesting resource tension.

### BUSHRANGER - Starting Deck (10 cards)

Examples only
**Basic Cards:**

- 3x **Shoot**: 6 damage, 1 cost
- 2x **Defend**: 5 block, 1 cost
- 2x **Quick Draw**: 4 damage, If turn 1: draw 1, 1 cost

**Unique Starters:**

- 1x **Reload**: Draw 1, 1 cost
- 1x **Bounty Mark**: 5 damage, If this kills: gain 10 gold, 1 cost
- 1x **Duck and Cover**: 4 block, 1 cost

### Bushranger Specialist Cards
Examples only

**Common Class Cards:**

- **Fan the Hammer**:Deal 3 damage 3 times, 2 cost
- **Ambush**: If turn 1: 15 damage, else: 5 damage, 2 cost
- **Tracking Shot**: 7 damage, Reveal top card of draw pile, 1 cost
- **Standoff**: Both you and enemy skip next turn, 1 cost

**Rare Class Cards:**

- **Dead Eye**: Your attack cards deal double damage (Power) if your sanity is over 50%, 2 cost
- **Infamous**: +1 damage for each elite killed this run (Power), 3 cost
- **High Noon**: For 3 turns, all cards cost 0 but you can only play 1 per turn, 2 cost

**Unique Mechanic - Momentum**: Killing enemies quickly gives momentum tokens that carry to next fight, encouraging aggressive play.

### PUBLICAN - Starting Deck (10 cards)
Examples only

**Basic Cards:**

- 3x **Serve**: 3 damage, 3 block, gain 1 Brew, 1 cost
- 2x **Defend**: 5 block, 1 cost
- 2x **Ferment**: Do nothing, Fermented: 10 damage, 1 cost

**Unique Starters:**

- 1x **Happy Hour**: All cards cost 0 this turn, gain Hangover, 2 cost
- 1x **Share a Round**: You and enemy both heal 5, 1 cost
- 1x **Liquid Courage**: 8 damage, 8 block, gain Hangover, 2 cost

### Publican Specialist Cards
Examples only

**Common Class Cards:**

- **Bar Fight**: Damage = 2x Brew tokens, lose all Brew, 1 cost
- **Drinking Contest**: Whoever has less HP draws 2 cards, 1 cost
- **Last Orders**: Triple all Brew generation this combat, Exhaust, 2 cost
- **Nightcap**: End your turn, Gain 10 block, 1 cost

**Rare Class Cards:**

- **Established Regular**: Heal 2 HP per turn at settlements (Power), 1 cost
- **Vintage Collection**: All Fermented cards permanently upgraded (Power), 3 cost
- **Social Network**: +1 card draw at settlements/pubs/camps (Power), 2 cost

**Unique Mechanic - Fermentation**: Cards that get stronger if you don't play them for X turns.
